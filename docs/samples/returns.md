## Return window
You can return products within 30 days of delivery. The 30 days start on the day the parcel is delivered, not the day you ordered.

## Opened products
For hygiene reasons, opened food and supplement products (protein powders, capsules, bars with a broken wrapper) cannot be returned unless they are faulty or damaged on arrival.

## Faulty or damaged products
If a product arrives damaged, spoiled, or past its best-before date, report it within 14 days of delivery with a photo of the product and the batch number. We refund or replace it, and you do not pay return shipping.

## How to return
Request a return label through the customer account. Returns of unopened products cost 5.95 EUR, deducted from the refund. Returns of faulty products are free.

## Refunds
Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 14 days after the returned parcel arrives at our warehouse. Original shipping costs are refunded only if the whole order is returned.
