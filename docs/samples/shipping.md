## Delivery countries
We deliver to Germany, Austria, and the Netherlands. We do not ship outside these three countries.

## Shipping costs
Shipping within Germany costs 4.95 EUR. Orders of 50 EUR or more ship free within Germany. Shipping to Austria and the Netherlands costs 8.95 EUR regardless of order value.

## Delivery times
Orders placed on working days before 14:00 are dispatched the same day. Delivery within Germany usually takes 1 to 3 working days, and to Austria and the Netherlands 3 to 5 working days.

## Heat-sensitive products
From June to August, chocolate-coated protein bars are shipped with a cooling pack. We cannot guarantee that coated bars arrive without melting during heatwaves.

## Lost parcels
If tracking shows no movement for 7 working days, contact customer service. We open an investigation with the carrier and send a replacement or refund once the parcel is confirmed lost.
