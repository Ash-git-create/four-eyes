## Where allergen information comes from
Allergen information shown for each product comes from the manufacturer's label as recorded in Open Food Facts. The label on the product you receive always takes priority. If the two differ, follow the label and tell customer service.

## Declared allergens and traces
"Contains" means the allergen is an ingredient. "May contain traces of" means the product is made on lines or in facilities that also handle that allergen. A product with a traces warning is not suitable for people with a severe allergy to that allergen.

## Milk and lactose
Whey and casein protein are made from milk and contain milk protein. "Lactose-free" on a label means less than 0.1 g lactose per 100 g; it does not mean milk-free, so it is not suitable for people with a milk protein allergy.

## Gluten
Only products labelled "gluten-free" contain 20 mg/kg gluten or less. Oats and oat-based bars are not gluten-free unless labelled so.

## We do not give medical advice
Customer service cannot tell you whether a product is safe for your specific allergy or medical condition. For that, ask a doctor or allergist.
